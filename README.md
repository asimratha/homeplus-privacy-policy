# Privacy Policy for Home+ Launcher

**Last Updated:** August 31, 2026

Home+ Launcher ("Home+", "we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our Android application.

### SUMMARY
Home+ Launcher is designed with a strict **"Privacy-First & Zero Data Collection"** philosophy. We DO NOT collect, store, track, sell, or transmit any personal data, usage statistics, or analytics to any remote server or third party.

---

### 1. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT
* **No Personal Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, or device IDs.
* **No Tracking or Analytics:** Home+ contains NO analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or telemetry services).
* **No Advertising:** Home+ is 100% ad-free and contains no ad networks.
* **No Crash Uploads:** Crash reports and bug data remain strictly on your local device and are never uploaded to any remote server.

---

### 2. ON-DEVICE DATA & PERMISSIONS USED
All permissions requested by Home+ Launcher are used solely on your local device to provide standard launcher features:

* **Installed Applications (`QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`):**
  * *Purpose:* To display your installed apps in the App Drawer, create home screen shortcuts, and manage hidden apps.
  * *Data Handling:* This list stays strictly on your device and is never shared.

* **Storage / Photos & Media:**
  * *Purpose:* To allow you to pick custom wallpapers for the desktop/drawer and to export/import your launcher settings backup (`.homeplusbackup`).
  * *Data Handling:* Files are read and written strictly on your local storage.

* **Notification Access (Optional):**
  * *Purpose:* To display notification badges/dots on app icons and show media controls on the Smartspace widget.
  * *Data Handling:* Notification contents are processed locally in real time and are never recorded or stored.

* **Accessibility Service (Optional):**
  * *Purpose:* Exclusively used for the "Double Tap to Sleep" gesture to lock the screen without requiring root permissions.
  * *Data Handling:* The Accessibility Service does not observe or collect your keystrokes, screen content, or user interactions.

* **Location (Optional):**
  * *Purpose:* If enabled, used locally to display accurate weather information on the Smartspace (At a Glance) widget.
  * *Data Handling:* Location coordinates are used only for local weather requests and are not tracked or saved.

---

### 3. DATA SECURITY & BACKUPS
* All launcher configurations, desktop arrangements, hidden apps preferences, and color selections are stored locally in your device's private sandbox.
* When you create a backup, it is saved as a structured local file (`.homeplusbackup`) on your device. You have full control over where this file is stored.

---

### 4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
* Home+ Launcher does not integrate with any third-party tracking or advertising SDKs.
* If you use the integrated Search Bar to search the web (e.g., via Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, or Brave), your search query is processed directly by your chosen search provider and is subject to that provider's privacy policy.

---

### 5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Home+ Launcher does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

---

### 6. CONTACT US
If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the Home+ development team.
